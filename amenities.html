Amenities

Pool and Outdoor Recreation Area

Escape to a private outdoor oasis designed for both relaxation and social gatherings. Take a refreshing swim in the pool, unwind on the spacious patio, or enjoy a meal with family and friends at the community barbecue area. Comfortable outdoor seating creates the perfect setting to relax, entertain, and enjoy Honolulu’s year-round tropical climate.

Fitness Center

Stay active without leaving the community. Our fully equipped fitness center provides residents with a convenient space to maintain a healthy lifestyle, featuring a variety of cardio and strength-training equipment suitable for workouts of all fitness levels.
