About Royal Capitol Plaza

Located in the Heart of Kaka'ako, Royal Capitol Plaza is an established residential high-rise situated near Downtown Honolulu. Completed in 1897, the condominium is home to over 200 residences. Royal Capitol Plaza offers the convenience of urban living, providing easy access to business districts, shopping, dining, recreation, and many more destinations that make Kaka'ako one of Honolulu's most connected neighborhoods.

Royal Capitol Plaza includes a variety of floor plans and orientations with one or two bedroom options. Enjoy views of the Honolulu City Skyline, the Pacific Ocean, or Oahu's mountain range. The property is designed to support everyday living with secured community areas and recreational amenities.

Royal Capitol Plaza holds a distinctive place in history of modern Kaka'ako. Near the beginning of the Hawai'i Community Development Authority's (HCDA) transformation of the district, Royal Capitol Plaza was the first residential project approved by HCDA. It was among the first major residential towers constructed as part of Kaka'ako's planned redevelopment.

Construction began in 1986, during a time when Kaka'ako was characterized by low-rise commercial and industrial development. The 40-story tower was completed in 1987, marking the beginning of the neighborhood's transition toward the vibrant residential and mixed-use community that exists today. Since then, the building has remained a recognizable landmark in the Honolulu City Skyline.
